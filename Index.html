Privacy Policy

We collect only what's needed to run tournaments: game name, game ID, UPI ID, payment reference, and basic device identifiers.

Data is used to verify entries, run matches, and prevent fraud. We don't sell your data.

Payment details are used solely to match your UTR to your entry — we never ask for card numbers or passwords.

You can request deletion of your data by contacting support below.

If you have any questions or requests regarding your privacy or personal data, please contact our support team.
